IRSS x Silva21 Expansion Mine
=============================

A public-safe planning mine of IRSS and Silva21 outputs, screened against the FRESH forest modelling stack, with a prioritised integration queue and traceable public provenance.

This site is the RTFM surface for the project. Agents read the relevant page before
they act, and cite it in their reports. Start with the repository's ``AGENTS.md`` and
``ROADMAP.md``. The candidate register lives in ``registry/candidates.yaml``; the
generated browse view is ``docs/candidates.md``.
