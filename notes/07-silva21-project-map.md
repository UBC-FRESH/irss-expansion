## How to read the relevance column

- **H** — directly changes a FRESH model, data contract, or decision surface.
- **M** — useful evidence or a component, but not sufficient on its own.
- **L** — context, engagement, or a data layer with no near-term model path.

The project codes are Silva21 administrative codes, not priorities. Several codes appear twice because the programme has paired projects under the same number (for example, two `OB 7` projects: climate-sensitive growth in Ontario and regeneration failure in the boreal).
